🕸️ Swing: hold the left mouse button or Space, and let go at the bottom of the swing to launch.

📱 On a phone, drag the right side to look, use the left stick to steer, and tap the buttons.

📱 Tilt mode: tilt your phone to look and steer, and tap SWING. ✋ Hand tracking: move your hand to aim. Make a fist to swing, open your hand to let go, show two fingers ✌️ to shoot, and point 👆 to zip.
